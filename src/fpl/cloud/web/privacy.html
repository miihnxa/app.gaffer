Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 · Last updated 1 September 2026

Gaffer holds an email address and a Fantasy Premier League team ID. That is the whole of it. This policy explains why, how long, and what you can make us do about it.

1. Who is responsible

Gaffer is operated by A. Manager, Montreal, Quebec, Canada. For anything in this policy — including a request to see or delete your data — contact app.gaffer@gmail.com.

Under the UK and EU GDPR we are the data controller for the information described below.

2. What we collect, and why

DataWhyLegal basis
Email addressTo sign you in and send your 6-digit code. It is your account identifier.Performance of a contract

Your FPL team ID is public information. Anyone can look up any team ID on the official Fantasy Premier League website and see that squad, its manager name and its leagues. An account does not make your team private, and we would be misleading you if we implied otherwise.

What the account actually protects is your email address, your saved preferences, and the fact that nobody else's install of Gaffer opens on your team.

3. What we do not collect

  • No password. Not yours for Gaffer, and never your Fantasy Premier League password. We authenticate with emailed codes, so there is no password to leak.
  • No payment data. Gaffer is free. We hold no card or billing information at all.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking. No third-party trackers, no advertising identifiers, no behavioural profiling.
  • No access to your FPL account. We cannot sign in as you, make transfers, or change your team, and the app is built so that it never could.
  • No contacts, files, location or device identifiers.

4. Who else sees it

We do not sell, rent or share your data. It is handled only by the service providers needed to run Gaffer, each acting as a processor on our instructions:

  • Our hosting provider — runs the account service and its database.
  • Our email provider — delivers your sign-in code. It sees your email address.

We read public data from the Fantasy Premier League API. We never send them anything about you: they cannot tell that you use Gaffer.

5. Where your data is held

Our servers may be located outside your country, including in Canada, the United States or the European Union. Where data leaves the UK or EEA, we rely on the UK/EU Standard Contractual Clauses or an adequacy decision. If you would like to know the current hosting region, ask and we will tell you.

6. How long we keep it

  • Account data — for as long as your account exists.
  • Sign-in codes — 15 minutes, or until used. Whichever comes first.
  • Dormant accounts — deleted after 24 months without a sign-in.
  • Deletion requests — acted on immediately; backups age out within 30 days.

7. Your rights

Under the UK and EU GDPR, and under Canadian federal and Quebec law, you may:

  • Access — get a copy of everything we hold about you.
  • Rectify — correct anything wrong.
  • Erase — delete your account and its data. There is a button for this in the app under Settings, and it takes effect immediately.
  • Port — receive your data in a machine-readable format.
  • Object or restrict — tell us to stop processing.
  • Withdraw consent — at any time, without giving a reason.

Email app.gaffer@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days. You will never be charged for exercising a right.

If you are unhappy with our response you may complain to your data protection authority: the ICO in the UK (ico.org.uk), your national authority in the EU, the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada, or the Commission d'accès à l'information in Quebec.

8. Security

  • All traffic is encrypted in transit over HTTPS.
  • Sign-in codes are stored as salted HMAC-SHA256 hashes, never in plain text.
  • Codes expire in 15 minutes, work once, and are destroyed after five wrong attempts.
  • Session tokens are signed and expire after 90 days.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach ever affects your data we will tell you and the relevant regulator without undue delay, and within 72 hours where the GDPR requires it.

9. Children

Gaffer is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes

If we change this policy materially we will email account holders before it takes effect. The version number and date at the top always reflect the current text.
