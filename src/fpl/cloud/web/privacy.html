Privacy Policy

Last updated 1 September 2026

What we collect

Only what the service needs to work:

  • Your email address — to sign you in and to email you a receipt or an alert you asked for.
  • Your FPL team ID — a public number, so the app remembers which team to load.
  • Usage counts — how many lookups you made on a given day, to enforce the free-tier limit.

What we do not collect

We do not ask for or store your Fantasy Premier League password. We do not store card details — Stripe handles payment and we only keep the customer and subscription identifiers they return. We do not use advertising trackers, and we do not sell or share your data with anyone.

Passwords

There are none. Sign-in uses a single-use link emailed to you that expires in 20 minutes. We never store a password, so one can never leak.

Cookies

One cookie, gaffer_session, which keeps you signed in for 30 days. It is strictly necessary for the service to work. No analytics or advertising cookies.

Third parties

  • Stripe — payment processing. See Stripe's privacy policy.
  • Fantasy Premier League — we read public data from their endpoints. We do not send them anything about you.
  • Our host — servers and standard request logs.

Retention and your rights

We keep your account while it exists. Email us and we will delete your account and all associated data, permanently, within 30 days. You may also request a copy of everything we hold about you.
