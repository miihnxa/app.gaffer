What it does

Four things, done properly

Built on the official Fantasy Premier League data. No predictions, no black box — every call it makes is one you can check yourself.

Your squad, read properly

The XI on a pitch with fixture difficulty, injuries and flags, blank gameweeks, illegal formations, bad bench order, and players of yours facing each other.

The transfer guard

Every suggested replacement has already cleared your form rule, your bank, the three-per-club limit and your hit rules. If nothing clears them, it says so instead of inventing a pick.

The squad rebuilder

Lock who you keep, set a bench budget, get a legal 15. It buys the bench first, so a Bench Boost is actually worth playing — most builders spend everything on the XI and leave you four £4.0m players.

Your mini-leagues

Standings, weekly movement, and one click into any rival's squad to see what they own that you don't.

Questions

The honest answers

Do I have to give you my FPL password?

No — and you never should, to anyone. Gaffer only needs your team ID, the number in the URL of your Points page. Everything it reads is public. It cannot sign in to your account and cannot change your team.

Can it make my transfers for me?

No, deliberately. Fantasy Premier League has no official write API, so any tool claiming to act for you is storing your login and driving the website. That's a credential risk and a good way to get an account flagged. Gaffer advises; you tap it in yourself.

Will it show transfers I've already made this week?

Not until the deadline passes. The public FPL data doesn't publish a squad for a gameweek that hasn't started, so nobody can see your pending team without your login. Gaffer tells you plainly which gameweek it's showing rather than pretending it's current.

Is this official?

No. Gaffer is independent and not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to the Premier League or Fantasy Premier League.
