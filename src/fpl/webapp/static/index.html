Welcome to Gaffer.

Enter any Fantasy Premier League team ID and Gaffer reads that squad from public data — flagged players, blank gameweeks, fixture difficulty, and the transfers that actually improve the team rather than just changing it.

It works for any manager in the game, so you can load a rival's team as easily as your own.

Where do I find my team ID?
  1. Sign in at fantasy.premierleague.com
  2. Open the Points tab
  3. Look at the address bar — it reads /entry/1234567/event/3
  4. The number after /entry/ is your team ID

A friend’s team: open any mini-league table you share and click their team name — the same /entry/… number appears in the address bar.
